Upon admission, learners’ academic progress is closely monitored using multiple tools and metrics. These include trimestral gradesheets and class means reports, which consolidate performance across sections. One Nexus, the school’s integrated online platform, provides a centralized source of academic information, giving parents real-time access to detailed scores, grades, and distinctions such as honors and awards. To address academic concerns early, a Students Needing Intervention file tracks learners with missed assessments or other issues. This file is mirrored per grade level from a central spreadsheet, allowing only the grade-level teachers to access it while enabling timely coordination for interventions. Additionally, Lexile and Quantile assessment results (Appendix 4.13.26; Appendix 4.13.27) monitor reading and mathematical proficiency to support targeted interventions.


Additionally, One Nexus, the school’s integrated online platform, provides parents with real-time academic updates, including grades, honors, and the Hibernon Award. The Hibernon Award recognizes students who demonstrate at least an 8-point increase in academic performance between two grading periods, provided they have a deportment grade of ‘S’ or higher (Appendix 4.13.28 “One Nexus”).
